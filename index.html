Make the complex feel simple. Make the value feel real.

I partner with revenue teams to bridge the gap between complex technical products and the business outcomes customers care about. I specialize in deals that need more than a polished deck: multi-stakeholder environments, deep API conversations, and buyers who need to trust the solution before they sign.

My roots are in implementation, which is why I still think like the team that has to deliver. Today that shows up as presales, post-sales, and forward deployed work in one role, with AI handling the busywork so I can stay focused on the customer.
